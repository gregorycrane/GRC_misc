About this edition

This edition contains every scan page from both supplied volumes, including Latin, English, front matter, notes and indexes. Volume I records a revision in 1951. Page pairs retain the source layout: English footnotes often appear below the facing Latin text. Their position on a source page is preserved; no attachment to a particular word is inferred.

The optional flowing view changes display whitespace only. It retains printed line-end hyphens because joining them automatically could change compound words. This is not yet a fully proofread or chapter-and-section encoded edition.
