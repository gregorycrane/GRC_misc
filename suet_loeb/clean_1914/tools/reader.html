About this edition

Both title pages of the supplied PDF identify 1914. This edition retains all 1,094 PDF pages. Fresh OCR was generated locally because the PDF’s embedded text is defective. Page images preserve the printed wording and note positions; OCR remains subject to proofreading.

Gemini supplements fill the untranslated chapters 43–44. Chapters 45–47 are already translated by Rolfe; the inherited Gemini-attributed versions of those chapters remain available separately as archival alternatives. The 1970 OCR edition remains a separate comparison witness.
